HEARTLAB

Exploring how lifestyle risk factors relate to coronary heart disease deaths across U.S. states.

HEARTLAB is an educational modeling tool. It examines associations between lifestyle risk factors and coronary heart disease mortality across U.S. states, using historical state-level data and a multiple linear regression model.

Its results describe population-level statistical relationships, not an individual’s medical risk. Associations do not establish causation, and simulated estimates depend on the model’s assumptions and on the values entered.

It is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
